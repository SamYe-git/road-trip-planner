Palette

Pencil on drawing paper, plus the truck's own colors: body red, saddle-tan cloth and charcoal from the cab, and the gold side stripe used only for highlights. Blue only for water and weather.

Type

Big condensed capitals for names and headings (the adventure-site feel), handwriting for things you'd scribble on a map (leg times, weather), monospace for numbers.

Adding stops

Every leg has a dashed + pin. It opens a note card right in that gap: search a town, pick how long you stay, add it. The leg splits in two and every time below it updates. Middle stops get a small × to remove them.

Motion

One moment only: the map unfolds when you click it. While a route loads, the truck drives across the road at the top.

Phone

The cab scales down as one picture. On the map, the stop cards widen, the zig-zag stays, and the mountains thin to narrow strips at the edges.

Example trip for the sketch only (Dallas to Boston). Times and miles are rounded placeholders, not API results.
